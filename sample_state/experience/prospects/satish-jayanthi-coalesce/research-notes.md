- It connects to the campaign's reason: Coalesce operates in the Snowflake ecosystem where enterprise customers (financial services, healthcare, regulated verticals) demand SOC 2 evidence + access lineage on a continuous cadence. Adding Quality/Catalog to the platform increases the security surface area and makes the question of "what does continuous compliance look like across this platform" more urgent, not less.

**How the email opens with the trigger:** Reference Satish's own framing about trust in the data stack — specifically his "tap water" metaphor — and bridge from data quality trust to infrastructure security trust: when engineers build with confidence, and analysts trust the data, the next question a CTO at a regulated-industry-serving platform has to answer is whether the security controls governing who touches that data are as continuously verified as the data quality is. That is the gap Transilience closes.
