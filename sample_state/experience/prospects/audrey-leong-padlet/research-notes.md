For Audrey, this means the inbound questionnaire volume from districts is growing every year, not flattening.

**Trigger framing:** The Arcade launch is the sharper opener (specific, recent, product-side). The state law stack is the backdrop that makes the opener land — it contextualizes the workload, not just the new product.
