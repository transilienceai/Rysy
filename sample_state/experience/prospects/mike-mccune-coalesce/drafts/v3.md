**v2 → v3 changes (driven by user-level feedback):**

1. **Subject line out of vendor jargon, into engineer vocabulary.** "After CastorDoc and SYNQ, the compliance surface" was vendor-coded — "compliance surface" is a phrase a vendor uses, not a phrase a VP Eng uses internally. "SOC 2 after two acquisitions" is the way Mike (or any engineering leader at a SaaS that just acquired twice) actually thinks about this in their head. Lower mental-translation cost on the open.

2. **Replaced the duplicated four-item product line with Mike's frame-specific phrasing.** v2 used "declared baseline, continuous drift detection, remediation human-in-the-loop" — the same line that appeared in Satish's and Justin's drafts. v3 reframes the product mention in engineering-hours/byproduct-of-running terms: "Transilience generates the evidence as a byproduct of how the cloud runs — the controls are continuously valid, not collected after the fact." This implicitly differentiates from Drata/Vanta-class compliance-automation tools (which collect evidence after the fact) without naming them as a positioning statement. Lives entirely in the engineering-economist frame Mike has shown publicly.

3. **Removed the "where that math shows up" presumption.** Mike did the math; he knows where it shows up. Replaced with "is one place that lands" — observation, not interpretation.

4. **Dropped the "worth fifteen minutes to compare" templating.** Several other Coalesce drafts had a "worth fifteen minutes" CTA shape; the open question is enough as the CTA here. A reply with a one-line answer is a better next step than a calendar request.
