**Subject:** IaC declared vs actual after two acquired stacks

**Body:**

Two acquired stacks absorbed in twelve months, multi-cloud Kubernetes at scale, the same small infra team — the gap between what Terraform declares and what the cloud actually looks like widens between audit cycles in ways that aren't visible until they accumulate.

Closing it is mostly a declare-the-baseline-and-watch-it-continuously problem, additive to the existing observability stack rather than replacing any of it.

Transilience runs the continuous side across IaC, cloud config, and identity together — declared baseline, drift detection, prioritized findings, remediation human-in-the-loop.

Worth fifteen minutes if the framing maps to where the surface is currently expanding fastest?
