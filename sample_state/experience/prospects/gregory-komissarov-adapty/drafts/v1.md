**Subject:** PSP credential state on self-hosted k8s

**Body:**

Six PSPs absorbed into a self-hosted k8s cluster in twelve days is the kind of integration where the credential-state of each PSP — keys, rotation, scoped roles, secret ages — accretes faster than anyone declares a baseline against.

Transilience does that part — IAM policy, credential rotation, and cluster-side secret scoping continuously baselined, additive to whatever observability already runs.

How is credential state currently tracked at the cluster level?
