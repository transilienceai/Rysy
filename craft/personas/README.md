# craft/personas/

The buyer archetypes Rysy writes to. Seeded from the team's ABM doc; extended by Rysy as she observes patterns in real prospects.

## Format

Every persona file follows the schema in `format.md`. Consistency matters — the drafter and the profiler both consult these files, and inconsistent formats break their reasoning.

## How personas are used

When Rysy researches a prospect, the profiler sub-agent uses the relevant persona file to anchor the psychological portrait. When Rysy drafts, she pulls voice-register cues from the persona's *voice register fit* section. When the witness reviews, it consults the persona for *what this reader hates seeing in a cold email*.

## How personas evolve

Persona files start as the seeded version. As Rysy works, she takes craft notes about specific prospects who fit a persona, and over time those notes accumulate into observations that promote into the persona file itself. The persona file's *what they respond to* and *what they hate* sections are the most likely to grow.

The seeded sections — *who they are*, the role description, the typical career path — should not be casually rewritten. They are part of the calibration that lets the team and Rysy share a vocabulary about who we are reaching out to.
