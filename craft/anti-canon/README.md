# craft/anti-canon/

Bad cold emails dissected. Real examples of the work done badly. What Vendy is avoiding, made concrete.

## What an anti-canon entry is

An anti-canon entry is a real cold email — anonymized — that demonstrably failed, accompanied by a dissection of *why*. The format mirrors `craft/exemplars/` exactly, with the *why it fails* section replacing *why it works*.

Anti-canon makes failure concrete. Reading prose about what bad cold email looks like is weaker than reading actual bad cold email and seeing the failure modes named.

## Where anti-canon entries come from

**Your spam folder.** Real cold emails received by you and your team that exemplify the failure modes.

**Public dissection corpus.** Cold-email teardowns on Josh Braun's *Bad Cold Email of the Day*, Becc Holland's *Flip the Script* episodes, threads on r/sales calling out bad outreach. Provenance always credited.

**Vendy's own collection.** When she encounters one in the wild — a bad cold email arriving in any monitored mailbox — she may add it to anti-canon (with appropriate anonymization).

## How anti-canon is used

The witness consults anti-canon when a draft is borderline. The diagnostic question is: *does this draft look more like exemplar X or anti-canon Y?* Concrete failure cases sharpen the witness's judgment in ways that abstract rules do not.

Vendy also reads anti-canon during periodic introspection — to recalibrate against the failure modes she may be drifting toward.

## Anonymization rules

- Sender names and companies anonymized
- Identifying technical details abstracted if they would identify the sender
- Recipient names removed entirely
- The substance of the failure preserved verbatim — anonymization should not soften the failure

## Format

See `format.md`. Same as exemplars, with section names adjusted for failure framing.

## Why this directory exists separate from `cold-email/ai-tells-graveyard.md`

The graveyard is a list of phrases. Anti-canon is whole emails. They serve different purposes:

- The graveyard catches *specific tells* deterministically (via the hook)
- Anti-canon trains *judgment* about failures that operate at the whole-email level

A draft can avoid every phrase in the graveyard and still be an anti-canon-shaped failure. The two complement each other.
